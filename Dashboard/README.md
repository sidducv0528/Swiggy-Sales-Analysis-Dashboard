## Layout

The workbook is organized into three navigable views (buttons on the left rail): **Dashboard**, **Analysis**, and **Data**.

**KPI header** — five headline cards: Total Sales, Average Rating, Avg Order Value, Rating Count, Total Orders.

**Visuals on the Dashboard view:**
- Monthly Sales Trend (line chart, Jan–Aug)
- Sales by Food Type (Veg/Non-Veg donut, 65% / 35%)
- Sales by State (India map, shaded by sales)
- Daily Sales Trend (Sun–Sat bar chart)
- Daily Total Orders, Sales & Rating by quarter (Q1–Q3 table)
- Top 5 State Sales (Karnataka, Uttar Pradesh, Telangana, Maharashtra, Delhi)
- Weekly Sales Trend (bar chart across the full period)

**Interactive filters (slicers):**
- Months (Jan–Sep)
- State
- Restaurant Name

## How to Use

1. Download or open the [live workbook](https://1drv.ms/x/c/cbf615f6cf8b4d72/IQC3o8yVFT9aTYvkOBoxL9JiAXMfOFXEnnTPoxHrWiEOqeo?e=67fkd5) in Excel or Excel Online.
2. Use the **Months**, **State**, or **Restaurant Name** slicers to filter the entire dashboard.
3. Switch between the **Dashboard**, **Analysis**, and **Data** views using the left-hand navigation buttons.

For a guided tour instead of exploring it yourself, see the [video walkthrough](../Demo).
